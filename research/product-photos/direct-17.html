The agile professional for natural gardens, orchards, and open spaces – our solo by AL-KO BM 5001-R IV sickle bar mower

The solo® by AL-KO Premium BM 5001-R IV sickle bar mower, as a complete unit including the 102 cm wide cutter bar, combines everything you need for maintaining natural gardens, agricultural land, and producing forage grass: environmentally friendly cutting for natural areas, high performance, and the ability to cut through dense undergrowth.

Powerful performance – perfect handling

Easy manoeuvring on any terrain and slope thanks to the short wheelbase, large, wide tyres, and powerful wheel drive with forward and reverse gears.

The ergonomic, vibration-damped handlebar, adjustable to any height, and the switchable wheel drive ensure comfortable and safe operation of the sickle bar mower.

The 102 cm cutting width cutter bar, included with the complete unit, features a separately switchable cutting drive for your safety while working. It is equipped with a specially hardened, high-quality blade from ESM – made in Germany.

Independent and extremely manoeuvrable, the BM 5001-R IV sickle bar mower quickly and efficiently handles all mowing tasks with its 102 cm cutting width. The powerful gasoline engine and wheel drive ensure easy access and a reliable cut, even in tall brush, dense vegetation, on uneven terrain, and on slopes. The machine's exceptional manoeuvrability makes it ideal for use along virtually any meadow edge or field boundary.

The powerful 2.7 kW engine ensures fast and consistent operation. It powers the switchable blade drive as well as the equally switchable wheel drive, which features forward and reverse gears. With its individually controlled drive system, the mower truly shines even on rough terrain with winding edges (e.g., on sloping meadows), ensuring exceptional safety. Combined with the height-adjustable, vibration-damped handlebar for perfect, fatigue-free operation and the 13-inch tyres with a farmer's tread, optimal handling is possible on virtually any terrain, while also minimising strain on the body. The movable grass deflector prevents grass clippings from accumulating on the collection tray.

Buy the solo® by AL-KO BM 5001-R IV & Alternatives

You can buy the BM 5001-R IV here from our online webshop or from one of our authorised dealers. If you prefer a sickle bar mower with a narrower cutting width, we recommend the BM 875 IV (item no. 114098).
