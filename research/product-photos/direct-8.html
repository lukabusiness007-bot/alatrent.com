KombiSystem Attachments

KB-KM Bristle Brush Attachment
$319.99
Current Product Selection
  • Product Details
    • Bristle brush for the STIHL KombiSystem
    • For use on paved areas or uneven stone floors
    • Removes dirt from the deepest grooves and furrows
    • Firm bristles and backward-rotating brushes improve efficiency

This STIHL YARD BOSS® bristle brush attachment makes quick work of sweeping jobs on walkways and other paved surfaces.

Give big cleanup jobs a clean finish with the STIHL bristle brush attachment for the YARD BOSS® yard care system. Durable nylon bristles and a wide sweeping surface clear up to 24 inches in a single pass, making quick work of leaves on walkways and driveways. It’s also ideal for fluffing artificial turf, giving you another practical way to keep outdoor spaces looking their best.

For added convenience and maneuverability, pair the bristle brush attachment with the optional MM Wheel Kit. With broad coverage and durable construction, it adds even more versatility to the STIHL YARD BOSS® for cleanup around your property.

NOTE: STIHL KombiMotors and STIHL KombiSystem attachments are sold separately.

NOTE: This attachment must be used with large deflector and extensions. Do not use on grass lawns.
