    Article description

    The Einhell TC-WI 500 hand winch can handle a maximum payload of 500 kilograms. The hand winch from Einhell has a 10 meter twist-free wire cable (Ø 4.2 mm) with a load hook and a retention latch. The retention latch prevents the load from being unhooked accidentally. Thanks to a return stop the cable and the load can be stopped securely. The hand crank has a soft grip for a firm, secure hold and a comfortable working. There is a mounting plate with holes for quick and easy fixation.
