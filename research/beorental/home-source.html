Types of Air Therapy Devices:

  1. Air Purifiers – Remove dust, allergens, and pollutants using HEPA, carbon, or ionization filters.
  2. Ozone Generators – Disinfect air by breaking down bacteria, viruses, and odors.
  3. Humidifiers – Increase moisture levels in dry environments to prevent respiratory issues.
  4. Dehumidifiers – Reduce excess humidity, preventing mold and dust mite growth.
  5. UV Air Sanitizers – Use ultraviolet light to kill airborne pathogens.
  6. Aromatherapy Diffusers – Release essential oils for relaxation and respiratory benefits.
  7. Negative Ion Generators – Improve air freshness and potentially reduce stress.
  8. Ventilation Systems with Air Filtration – Ensure continuous air circulation while filtering contaminants.
